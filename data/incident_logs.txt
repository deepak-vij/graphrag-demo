=== Log Entry 2026-09-10 03:12 UTC | Source: infra-alerts ===
The Authentication Database experienced a disk volume failure at 03:04 UTC. On-call engineer
Priya Nair was paged. The Auth DB runs on host db-auth-03 in the us-east-1 region.

=== Log Entry 2026-09-10 03:15 UTC | Source: service-mesh ===
Login Service began returning HTTP 500 errors starting at 03:06 UTC. Login Service depends on
the Authentication Database for credential lookups. Error rate climbed to 92% within four minutes.

=== Log Entry 2026-09-10 03:18 UTC | Source: service-mesh ===
Checkout Service reported cascading failures at 03:09 UTC. Checkout Service calls Login Service
to validate session tokens before allowing purchases. Because Login Service was failing, Checkout
Service could not confirm any user sessions.

=== Log Entry 2026-09-10 03:22 UTC | Source: billing-team ===
The Payments Svc, owned by the billing team, logged a 78% drop in successful transactions between
03:09 and 03:40 UTC. Payments Svc is invoked by Checkout Service as the final step of the purchase
flow. No direct fault was found in Payments Svc itself.

=== Log Entry 2026-09-10 03:25 UTC | Source: analytics ===
Revenue Dashboard flagged an anomalous 61% drop in completed orders during the incident window.
Revenue Dashboard aggregates completed-order events emitted by the Payment Service after a
successful charge.

=== Log Entry 2026-09-10 04:02 UTC | Source: infra-alerts ===
Engineer Priya Nair restored the Auth Database by failing over to replica db-auth-04. Login
Service recovered within two minutes of the failover completing.

=== Log Entry 2026-09-10 04:05 UTC | Source: service-mesh ===
Checkout Service error rates returned to baseline at 04:04 UTC, immediately after Login Service
recovered, confirming Checkout Service's dependency on Login Service.

=== Log Entry 2026-09-10 09:00 UTC | Source: postmortem-bot ===
Postmortem PM-2216 was opened by Priya Nair. Root cause: Authentication Database disk failure.
Blast radius: Login Service, Checkout Service, Payment Service completions, Revenue Dashboard
reporting. PM-2216 recommends adding a secondary replica for the Auth DB in us-east-1.
